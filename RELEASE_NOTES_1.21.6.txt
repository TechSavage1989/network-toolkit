Network Toolkit 1.21.6 Beta 7

## What's New

- Terminal output can now be colored with keyword rule sets like MobaXterm's. In Display, choose Syntax highlighting: Standard keywords (errors, warnings, success and more), Unix shell script, Network configuration, Perl or SQL. The device highlighting you know stays the default.
- Customize lets you copy a rule set and set the words or patterns for each color, try them on your own text, and import or export MobaXterm .msyn files.

## Additional Details

- Each color (Red, Green, Yellow, Blue, Magenta, Cyan and Underline) takes a regular expression or a comma-separated word list, with a case-sensitive option. MobaXterm patterns work as written, including their bracket style and line markers.
- Imported files are named after the file. Built-in rule sets are read-only; your own rule sets save as you type, and up to 50 can be kept.
- Patterns slow enough to freeze the terminal are refused with an explanation; invalid patterns are shown in red and skipped.
- Colors follow the terminal color scheme. Text the device already colored keeps its color. Local PowerShell and Command Prompt tabs keep their own shell colors.
- Your rule sets and choice are kept after a restart and in separate Terminal windows.
- The full plan for MobaXterm-style terminal settings (fonts, colors and cursor, advanced options, session logging, more local shells) is in docs/TERMINAL_SETTINGS_PLAN.md and will arrive in the next betas.
- Validation used unit tests, browser checks and sample output; no real device was used, and it has not yet been checked on a physical Windows PC.
- Saved targets, connection profiles, credential vault, macros, unsaved documents, sessions and settings are kept. Security confirmations and Windows/UAC approval are unchanged.
- This is an opt-in beta. Stable users stay on 1.20.16. Portable executable filename remains Network_Toolkit.exe.
