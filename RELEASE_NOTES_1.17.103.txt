Network Toolkit 1.17.103

Condensed RTT graphs use a fixed nonlinear recent-time scale from startup. Adding older history no longer renormalizes recent points. Older measurements merge into one fixed-size historical cluster at the visible left plot boundary, while the newest point stays on the right boundary. Sparse startup leaves unused history space rather than stretching the curve.
The cluster uses bounded summaries accumulated before live-event and completed-sample retention rollover. Counts, recorded range, RTT extrema/average, zeros and unsuccessful samples survive without keeping an unlimited raw archive. Details distinguish retained originals from summarized-only data; legacy runs without summaries and partial archived ranges remain explicit.
Graph/grid/hit geometry shares stable plot boundaries, with rendering room outside the plot for endpoint markers. RTT lines and successful matching markers follow the selected accent immediately; failure, warning and non-RTT colors stay distinct.
Linear, deliberate time navigation, held selections, the proportional overview, recorded exports, pinned Local Network filters and updater behavior are preserved.
