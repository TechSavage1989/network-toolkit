Network Toolkit 1.21.18

## What's New

- The Terminal's Tools menu is gone. Everything it held already had a better place, so the toolbar is simpler.
- Password manager and SSH host keys are now at the bottom of the Saved and Recent sidebar, next to your connections.

## Additional Details

- Saved connections: double-click one in the Saved sidebar to open it, or right-click it to edit, rename, duplicate or remove it. Open sessions are the tabs above the terminal, and recent connections are on the Recent tab.
- Pane shortcuts: the ⓘ button beside the pane layout picker lists them.
- Separate terminal window: open it from the "Start a terminal" card. A separate terminal window has its own theme, app settings and help buttons in its toolbar.
- Help is still on F1 and the ? button at the bottom of the app sidebar.
- Saved targets, connection profiles, credential vault, macros, unsaved documents, sessions and settings are kept. Security confirmations, Windows/UAC approval, explicit update consent and checksum verification are unchanged.
- Linux: the Linux packages (x86_64 and aarch64) remain a preview; they have not yet been confirmed on a physical Linux PC.
- Portable executable filename remains Network_Toolkit.exe.
- Validation uses hosted builds and synthetic fixtures; the owner checked 1.21.17 on Windows and reported that it looks good.
