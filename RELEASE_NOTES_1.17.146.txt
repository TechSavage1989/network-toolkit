Network Toolkit 1.17.146 Beta 10

## What's New

- File comparisons are less sensitive to individual lines briefly shifting while scrolling.

## Additional Details

- The original narrow-window scrolling motion regression remains reproducible at the same measured offsets as Beta 9; it is not fixed by this change.

- Comparison alignment uses a median painted-row correction when at least three matching source rows are visible, reducing the impact of a temporarily displaced row. For one or two rows, previous behavior is retained.
- The Editor keeps authoritative source text, selection, undo/redo and saved preferences unchanged. No network, credential, transfer, permission, or updater consent behavior changes.
- The historical 390px map-motion, sidebar and source-alignment integration assertions remain release acceptance checks, not waived requirements. Native SFTP interruption and physical Windows endurance testing are still outstanding.
- This is an opt-in beta. The stable channel remains v1.17.136. Network_Toolkit.exe retains its fixed filename and update installation requires user approval.
