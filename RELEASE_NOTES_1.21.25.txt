Network Toolkit 1.21.25 Beta 1

## What's New

- Gray spacer rows keep matching lines level in editable large-file comparisons.
- FortiOS coloring reuses the visible highlight and waits for scrolling to settle before repainting.
- The change map defaults to Dynamic fit and remembers your last zoom. More Compare controls are available by right-clicking either pane.

## Additional Details

- Spacer rows are display only. Edits, clipboard operations and saves use source text and source line numbers.
- Show only differences includes navigation between added, removed and modified blocks.
- This beta is opt-in. Enable beta updates to receive it through the app.
