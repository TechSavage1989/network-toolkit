Network Toolkit 1.18.3 Beta 3

## What's New

- Find settings faster with a searchable Settings window, organized by topic.
- Press Ctrl+Shift+T to reopen a recently closed text document or an empty Terminal tab.
- Undo the removal of a saved diagnostic target from the notification.

## Additional Details

- Nine Settings categories organize General, Appearance, Terminal, Editor, Graphs, Transfers, Network, Updates and Confirmations. Existing update, vendor and storage controls retain their saved state and original authorization paths. Tool-specific settings remain available in their tools.
- Recently closed individual Editor tabs can be recovered with their in-memory contents, including unsaved changes, in the same window. Empty Terminal tabs can be recovered without reconnecting or transmitting credentials. This recovery history is limited to the current window, holds at most eight entries and is not persisted between app launches. It does not reopen closed live SSH, Telnet or Serial connections.
- Removing a saved diagnostic target has a short-lived Undo action. Destructive actions involving stored credentials, file overwrite, bulk live sessions, protected Windows networking and update download/install remain subject to existing safety confirmations, consent and checksum verification.
- Saved connection profiles, credential vault, settings, macros, network permissions, active connections, unsaved documents and source files retain their current storage and authorization boundaries. The Windows portable executable retains the filename Network_Toolkit.exe.
- This is an opt-in beta, not a stable release. Synthetic fixtures and hosted CI are not equivalent to physical Windows or live-device acceptance.
