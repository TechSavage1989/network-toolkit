Network Toolkit 1.17.67

What's new
- Manage hosts mappings in Entries and Text views, with aliases and comments.
- Add, edit, enable, disable, delete and filter entries individually or in bulk.
- Find invalid mappings, duplicate names and conflicting same-family addresses.
- Undo/redo draft changes; find/replace text and jump to a line.
- Preview, format, sort entry groups and remove exact active duplicates.
- Import/restore a text backup, append entries, or export a draft/loaded copy.
- Large files show 100 entries per page with selection across filtered pages.

Entry operations edit an in-memory draft. Review and save applies the reviewed
content through the existing protected Windows save process and creates its
normal backup. Existing revision checks, encoding/newline preservation and
administrator approval remain in place. The update system is unchanged.
