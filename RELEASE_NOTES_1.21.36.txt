Network Toolkit 1.21.36

## What's New

- Choose where to install Network Toolkit and whether to add a desktop shortcut.
- Open Toolkit, Text Editor, and Terminal independently from Start. Each has its own icon.

## Additional Details

- Setup reuses your chosen program folder on upgrade. Uninstall removes the installed app and its shortcuts while retaining saved settings, profiles, and documents.
- Files associated with Network Toolkit open in the standalone Text Editor, even while Toolkit is closed. The editor keeps its own preferences and supports large-file Compare and editing.
- Portable downloads and the verified binary-only updater remain available.
