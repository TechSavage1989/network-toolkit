Network Toolkit 1.17.72

What's new
- Organize saved SSH, Telnet, serial and FTP/FTPS sessions in named folders.
- Choose or create a folder when saving or editing a terminal session.
- Move existing sessions between folders or back to Unfiled.
- Filter saved sessions by folder and search by folder name.
- Folder assignments are kept when editing, duplicating and reopening profiles.

Folders follow the macro library's named-folder approach. Existing saved
sessions remain Unfiled until assigned. Connection settings, protected
credential links and startup macro bindings are retained. Folder names are
saved labels and never become filesystem paths or connection options.
