Network Toolkit 1.21.4 Beta 5

## What's New

- The ⇄ button that moves a sidebar to the other side is now at the bottom of every sidebar, as in the Text Editor: Terminal, File Transfer connections and server settings, and the Configuration panel.
- Because it sits on the sidebar's icon strip, you can move a sidebar even while it is hidden.

## Additional Details

- The Configuration panel has no icon strip while it is open, so ⇄ is in a slim row at the bottom of the panel; when the panel is hidden, ⇄ is at the bottom of its narrow strip, under the show button.
- The sidebar headings keep their titles and close buttons; nothing else moved.
- Validation used unit tests, browser tests and screenshots with sample data; it has not yet been checked on a physical Windows PC.
- Saved targets, connection profiles, credential vault, macros, unsaved documents, sessions and settings are kept. Security confirmations and Windows/UAC approval are unchanged.
- This is an opt-in beta. Stable users stay on 1.20.16. Portable executable filename remains Network_Toolkit.exe.
