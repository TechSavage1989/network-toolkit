Network Toolkit 1.21.34

## What's New

- Open, edit, compare, and save large text files. Word Wrap keeps original line numbers, and gray spacers keep matching lines aligned.
- Compare marks moved blocks and highlights changed values in logs, scripts, and device configurations.
- Hold Ctrl and roll the mouse wheel to zoom in the editor. Right-click for grouped Compare, Convert text, and Edit text commands.

## Additional Details

- The Stable release includes the detached Compare zoom alignment fix from v1.21.33 Beta 1. Matching rows stay aligned as the font changes with Word Wrap on or off.
- The large-file editor reads and saves in the background. Large files require temporary working space, and file operations depend on disk speed.
- Browser tests compare, edit, and save two synthetic 2 GiB files and verify the saved bytes.
