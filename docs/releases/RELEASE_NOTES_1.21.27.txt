Network Toolkit 1.21.27 Beta 1

## What's New

- Word Wrap works in the large-file editor and Compare. Continuation rows leave the gutter blank, and gray spacers keep matching lines aligned.
- More and Compare Options open beneath their buttons. Compare settings include navigation wraparound, changed-text selection in standard comparisons, and summary visibility.
- Text selection stays on the visible text. Compare keeps matching policy blocks aligned when new blocks contain repeated settings.

## Additional Details

- Wrap is applied to the loaded portion of the file. The scrollbar continues to navigate the full document.
- This beta is opt-in. Enable beta updates to receive it through the app.
