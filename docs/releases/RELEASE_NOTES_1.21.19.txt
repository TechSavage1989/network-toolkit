Network Toolkit 1.21.19

## What's New

- Terminal highlighting settings are now on the Appearance tab, right next to the preview. Pick a highlighting profile and see it in the preview straight away.
- While you scroll through the colors and options, the profile picker and preview stay in view, so every change shows as you make it.

## Additional Details

- Terminal settings now has three tabs: Appearance, Features, and Mouse & keyboard. Everything from the old Highlighting tab is on Appearance, in this order: syntax highlighting profile and preview, the profile's colors (click one to change it), device prompt name, underline options, Rules and colors, local shell colors.
- The font check sample (digits, box lines and Unicode) now sits right under the font settings.
- In short windows (under 640 pixels tall) the preview scrolls with the page instead of staying in view.
- Saved targets, connection profiles, credential vault, macros, unsaved documents, sessions and settings are kept, including your highlighting profile and colors. Security confirmations, Windows/UAC approval, explicit update consent and checksum verification are unchanged.
- Linux: the Linux packages (x86_64 and aarch64) remain a preview; they have not yet been confirmed on a physical Linux PC.
- Portable executable filename remains Network_Toolkit.exe.
- Validation uses hosted builds and synthetic fixtures.
