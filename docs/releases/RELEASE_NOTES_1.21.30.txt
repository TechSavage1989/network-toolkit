Network Toolkit 1.21.30 Beta 1

## What's New

- Compare keeps matching lines unshaded when a later match uses the same alignment.
- A long line replaced by a much shorter one shows a removal and an addition on the same aligned row. Smaller edits keep their changed-character highlighting.

## Additional Details

- The replacement markers appear in the editor panes and the differences-only view. The disk-backed change map shows the overall changed region.
- This beta is opt-in. Enable beta updates to receive it through the app.
