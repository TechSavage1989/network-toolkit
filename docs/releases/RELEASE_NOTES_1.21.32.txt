Network Toolkit 1.21.32 Beta 1

## What's New

- Hold Ctrl and use the mouse wheel over editor text to make it larger or smaller. Both panes zoom together in Compare and Split View.
- Compare keeps moved sections separate from adjacent edits so the changed words are easier to spot.

## Additional Details

- Comparison tests cover repeated lines, moved blocks, alignment spacers and changed text in synthetic logs and scripts.
- This beta is opt-in. Enable beta updates to receive it through the app.
