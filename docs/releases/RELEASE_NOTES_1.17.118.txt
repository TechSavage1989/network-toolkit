NETWORK TOOLKIT 1.17.118

CONSISTENT CONNECT SIDEBARS

- Connect is now above Files in the Text Editor's vertical sidebar. The selected
  panel and existing sidebar preferences remain unchanged.
- Every existing Connect sidebar tab uses one distinct connection-plug icon:
  Editor, transfer clients and transfer servers. Existing icon sizing, colors,
  labels, keyboard navigation, active states and connection controls are retained.
- Terminal Saved, Recent, Macros and SFTP tabs keep their own distinct symbols;
  unrelated serial/port-scan and settings icons are not repurposed.

This is a presentation-only update. It does not change connection, credential,
UAC, shell, transfer or document-ownership behavior. The Terminal tab click fixes
and CMD/PowerShell folder/admin controls remain unchanged. Existing narrow
comparison alignment, Hosts initial-focus and Alt-cross-window findings remain
recorded in docs/RELEASE117_HANDOFF.md; this update does not claim to fix them.
