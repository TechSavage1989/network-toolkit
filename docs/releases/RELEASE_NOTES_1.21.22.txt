Network Toolkit 1.21.22 Beta 1

## What's New

- The comparison map shows each changed line as a separate mark, even when changes are consecutive.
- Browse the map with the mouse wheel or its scrollbar, then click a mark to jump to that line.

## Additional Details

- Large-file comparisons load exact marks for the visible map area.
- Browser tests verified navigation to a change near the end of a 2 GiB file, including at 5× map zoom.
- This beta is opt-in. Enable beta updates to receive it through the app.
