Network Toolkit 1.21.72

## What's New

- While you compare two documents, the other side now follows when Find shows a match, so the lines stay side by side even after you scrolled the other side last.

## Additional Details

- This works in both kinds of comparison, for small and very large files.
- Replace All in All Opened Documents now shows the new text in a compared side that shows the same lines as the other side; before, that side could keep showing the old text.
- Saved targets, connection profiles, credential vault, macros, unsaved documents, sessions and settings are kept.
