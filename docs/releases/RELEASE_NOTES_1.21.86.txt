Network Toolkit 1.21.86

## What's New

- The packet capture traffic graph now shows plain bars of how many packets were recorded over time, with a caption saying how much time each bar covers.
- Hovering a packet in the list no longer opens a card over the list. It only marks that packet's bar in the graph.
- The maps beside the packet list use clearer colors. A color key above the list explains them, and each packet shows a matching color dot.

## Additional Details

- Bars start from zero, so their heights compare fairly. Hover a bar for its times, packets, bytes and rates, or click it to list its packets, as before.
- A small mark above a bar means some of its packets carry an error, warning or note.
- The graph's view controls moved onto the Traffic graph title row, leaving more room for the bars.
- Protocol colors you changed yourself in Colors & maps are kept; only the old default colors change.
- Saved targets, connection profiles, credential vault, macros, unsaved documents, sessions and settings are kept.
