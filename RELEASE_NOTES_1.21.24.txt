Network Toolkit 1.21.24 Beta 1

## What's New

- Use Compact or Dynamic fit to see more changes on the comparison map.
- Click a line to highlight its counterpart, or right-click a pane for comparison actions and changed-character highlighting.
- Each editor pane shows one vertical document scrollbar. FortiOS syntax coloring works in Single and Compare views.

## Additional Details

- Show only differences labels additions, removals, and modifications with their original line numbers.
- The disk-backed editor colors the loaded page without reading the whole file into the editor.
- This beta is opt-in. Enable beta updates to receive it through the app.
