Network Toolkit 1.21.88

## What's New

- Subnet masks are shown in full, such as 255.255.254.0 (/23), in Local network's IP configuration and on the Overview, instead of only the /23 prefix.

## Additional Details

- Current configuration lists the IPv4 address and its subnet mask separately, and the review before applying changes does the same.
- The Subnet mask or prefix length field starts with the full mask and still accepts either form, such as 255.255.254.0, 23 or /23.
- Copying an address from an Overview adapter card copies the address and its mask, such as 192.168.120.11 255.255.254.0.
- Saved targets, connection profiles, credential vault, macros, unsaved documents, sessions and settings are kept.
