Network Toolkit 1.19.7 Beta 7

## What's New

- Starting a multi-line paste no longer switches the Terminal's Macros panel to the Runs tab. You stay where you were; a short message still tells you that Pause and Stop are in Runs.
- Because the panel no longer jumps to Runs, it also stays put for macros you start afterwards.

## Additional Details

- Runs, Pause, Stop, Output and Clear are unchanged; open the Runs tab yourself when you want them.
- Saved targets, connection profiles, credential vault, macros, unsaved documents, sessions and settings are kept. Security confirmations and Windows/UAC approval are unchanged.
- This is an opt-in beta. Stable users stay on 1.19.0. Portable executable filename remains Network_Toolkit.exe.
- Validation uses hosted and synthetic fixtures; physical Windows and live-device acceptance is not formally claimed.
