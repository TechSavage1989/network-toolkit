Network Toolkit 1.21.20 Beta 1

## What's New

- Open, edit, compare and save multi-gigabyte text files without a fixed file-size or line-count limit.
- Large comparisons run in the background and let you jump between differences as they are found.
- Keep typing while a large file saves. Edits made after the save starts stay unsaved for your next save.

## Additional Details

- Large files use temporary disk storage and show only the section you are working on. Opening, searching, comparing and saving depend on disk or network speed. Large-file views use plain text and unwrapped lines.
- Large comparisons highlight changed lines and may group distant rearrangements into a single difference.
- The editor saves a consistent document revision and checks for outside changes before replacing a file. Format settings and undo history follow the document when it moves to another editor window.
- This beta is opt-in. Enable beta updates to receive it through the app.
